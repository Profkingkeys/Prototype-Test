1. Placing an order

Choose your items, sizes and quantities, provide accurate contact and delivery details, and review your request before submitting it. An order reference acknowledges receipt of your request. Each new order is pending review and unpaid until an admin reviews it and records the appropriate status. Submission does not automatically charge you, confirm availability or guarantee a delivery date.

2. Products, prices and availability

Prices are shown in Nigerian naira (₦). The item prices in your submitted order are retained in its record. Delivery charges are confirmed separately by the team. If a price, specification or availability needs correction, Virex will discuss it with you before you agree to proceed. Some garments are made to order. Size, fabric, embroidery and any custom measurements should be confirmed with the team before production.

Product photographs help you assess the garment. Campaign images provide styling inspiration, and screen settings may affect how colours appear. Ask the team for additional product details or photographs if a particular finish, colour or measurement is essential to your order.

3. Payment and manual updates

Arrange payment directly with Virex after the team confirms your order details and any delivery costs. This website does not collect card details or automatically process payments. Only an admin records whether an order is unpaid, awaiting confirmation, paid or refunded. Acceptance, preparation, dispatch, completion and stock adjustments are also performed manually. If a recorded payment or delivery status appears incorrect, contact the team with your order reference and relevant evidence.

4. Delivery and order tracking

Delivery method, charges and expected timing are agreed with the team for your location and order. Tracking reflects the updates entered by an admin; it is not live courier or GPS tracking. Keep your private tracking link securely. Anyone with that link can view the order’s items and progress. It does not display your address, phone number, email or internal admin notes.

5. Changes, cancellations, returns and refunds

Contact Virex promptly if you need to change or cancel an order, especially before production or dispatch. If an item is faulty, damaged, incorrect or does not match the agreed description, provide your order reference and explain the issue; photographs can help the team investigate. The appropriate repair, replacement, cancellation or refund will depend on the circumstances and applicable consumer rights. Any special conditions for custom work must be explained and agreed before you proceed.

Nothing in these terms removes any consumer right or remedy that applies under law. Information about Nigerian consumer rights is available from the Federal Competition & Consumer Protection Commission.

6. Your order and visit data

The name, contact details, address and notes you submit are used to review and fulfil your order and respond to related requests. Order management and customer details are restricted to approved admins. Product images are public storefront files stored separately from order records.

The site also records basic visit information such as a random browser ID, session activity, device/browser type, referring source and the section being viewed. It does not identify an anonymous visitor by their real name. A short-lived network hash is used to limit abuse. Statistics are private to admins. Contact Virex if you have questions about your submitted details or want to request a correction or deletion, subject to any records that must be retained.
