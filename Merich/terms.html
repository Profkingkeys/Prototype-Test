Terms & Conditions

Effective 26 October 2026

These terms explain how meal enquiries and order requests from this website are handled. Contact Merich Foods on WhatsApp at +234 708 527 7643 with questions about your meal or request.

1. Requesting a meal

Choose your meals and quantities, review your request, and provide your name, delivery area and any relevant notes. Continue on WhatsApp opens an editable message addressed to Merich Foods. You must review and send it in WhatsApp. Preparing or opening the draft does not itself place a confirmed order.

2. Prices, portions and availability

The menu invites you to ask for today’s price. Merich Foods confirms current prices, available meals, portion sizes, quantities and any special requests directly with you on WhatsApp before you agree to proceed. Food imagery presents the menu and styling; confirm the portion, ingredients and presentation you need with the kitchen.

3. Delivery and payment

Delivery options, fees, expected timing and payment arrangements are confirmed directly with Merich Foods for your area and request. This website does not collect card details or process payments. A prepared message is not a receipt, payment confirmation or delivery guarantee.

4. Changes or a problem with your meal

Contact Merich Foods promptly on WhatsApp if you need to change or cancel a request, correct delivery details or discuss a problem with a meal. Explain the issue and refer to your WhatsApp conversation so the team can review it. Any resolution depends on the circumstances and applicable consumer rights. These terms do not remove rights or remedies that apply under law.

5. Your information

Your selected meals and quantities may be saved in this browser so you can return to your request. Your name, delivery area and notes are kept in the current page’s memory while you prepare the message; they are not saved in the browser’s persistent request list. When you choose Continue on WhatsApp, the draft and the details you entered are passed to WhatsApp. Your subsequent conversation is handled through that service and the Merich Foods account.

This website does not create a customer account or store your request in an online order database. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or login codes in the notes. To discuss details already shared in a WhatsApp conversation, contact Merich Foods there.

6. Responsible use

Provide accurate details and use the ordering links for genuine enquiries and requests. Please do not send fraudulent orders, impersonate someone else or use the contact channel for spam. Confirm any important requirements directly with Merich Foods before accepting the order details.
